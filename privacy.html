Shawn Bot Email

Privacy policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Shawn Bot Email is a private application used by its owner to receive requested email notifications. It is not available for public registration.

Google account access

The application requests the Gmail send permission (gmail.send). It uses that permission to send the owner's requested notifications from the owner's Gmail account to the owner. It does not request permission to read, search, or delete inbox messages.

Information handled

The application handles its owner's email address, authorization tokens, notification content, and delivery receipts. Authorization tokens are kept in restricted files on the owner's server and are not included in application logs or on this website. Notification content is transmitted to Google Gmail for delivery to the owner.

Use, sharing, and retention

Information is used to authenticate the application, deliver requested notifications, and prevent duplicate messages. It is not sold, used for advertising, or provided to other users. The owner controls the notification records and delivery receipts retained on the server and the messages retained in Gmail.

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Owner controls and contact

The owner can disable notifications without stopping other software, revoke access through Google Account permissions, and remove locally retained records independently. Revoking authorization does not automatically delete emails already delivered or local delivery receipts. Contact information is shown on the application's Google consent screen.
