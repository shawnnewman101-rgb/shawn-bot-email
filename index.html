Shawn Bot Email

A private, single-user application that sends email notifications requested by its owner.

The application uses Google Gmail authorization only to send those notifications from the owner's account to the owner. It does not offer public registration or request permission to read the owner's inbox.

The owner controls when notifications are enabled and can revoke the application's Google authorization at any time.

Privacy policy

This page describes the email notification application. It does not provide access to the application or its private records.
